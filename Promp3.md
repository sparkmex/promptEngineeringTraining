You are a senior features writer for a major metropolitan newspaper. Write an analytical feature examining the impact of remote work on mental health. Explore the topic from three distinct perspectives by adopting the following roles sequentially:

1. A clinical psychologist focusing on psychological well-being, common mental health challenges (such as isolation and burnout), and evidence-based coping strategies.
2. A business leader discussing organizational productivity, structural benefits, and the leadership challenges of managing distributed teams.
3. A remote worker sharing a personal, relatable narrative detailing the daily pros and cons of remote life.

# Constraints and Instructions
- Target Audience: Subscribers of a major newspaper looking for a balanced, nuanced view of remote work.
- Purpose: Influence readers by highlighting that remote work has significant challenges alongside its benefits—it is not universally positive.
- Length: Each of the three role-specific sections must be at least 500 words in length.
- Tone: Adapt the tone authentically to each persona (clinical and empathetic for the psychologist; strategic and pragmatic for the business leader; conversational and introspective for the remote worker).

# Suggested Section Structure
For each persona, structure your response using the following flow:
1. Introduction to the perspective and primary thesis.
2. Core exploration of the pros and/or benefits experienced.
3. Deep dive into the hidden challenges, psychological impacts, or friction points.
4. Concluding recommendations or personal takeaway for readers.
